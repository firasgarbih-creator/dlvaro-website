01 /Founding identity

A watch can be more than a mechanism.
It can be a work of art.

DLVARO is an independent watch microbrand dedicated to the art of watch design — where creativity, visual identity, proportion, color, materials, and detail come together to create something unique to wear.

We do not define a watch by how complicated its movement is. We define it by how powerful its idea is.
