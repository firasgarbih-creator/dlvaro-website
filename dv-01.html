DV-01 / The dial

DIAL

Identity, in every facet.

The face of DV-01 brings together geometric relief, sculptural details, and a distinctive orange seconds hand. Each element belongs to one visual composition.

Every detail has its place.↓

A signature in three dimensions.

Repeating triangular facets give the dial its architectural character. Light and shadow reveal the pattern from different angles, carrying the geometric signature of DLVARO into the heart of the watch.

DV-01 / Light & shadow

Another expression after dark.

In the night-time visualization, green luminous accents trace the hands, selected hour markers, and the six-petal emblem. The same composition takes on a different presence against the dark dial.

01

Relief and rhythm

Raised hour markers sit above the triangular pattern. A dark outer minute track frames the composition and separates the reading of time from the textured dial.

02

The orange signature

The hour and minute hands have light-filled centres and sculpted outlines. A slender orange seconds hand introduces the accent colour of DV-01.

03

Two circular focal points

The six-petal DLVARO emblem occupies the left side, balanced by a circular date-window frame on the right. The DLVARO and AUTOMATIC plaques complete the arrangement.
